## 🧬 About Me

I'm a **B.Tech Biotechnology student at Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology & Research (VFSTR)** with a strong interest in applying computational tools to biological and biomedical challenges.

My work and learning sit at the intersection of:

- **Biotechnology & life sciences** — molecular biology, biochemistry, microbiology and bioprocess engineering.
- **Computational biology** — bioinformatics, molecular docking and computer-aided drug discovery.
- **AI & data science** — Python-based analysis, machine learning and AI-assisted biomedical applications.
- **Software development** — problem-solving, data structures and building practical tools.

I enjoy turning scientific questions into computational workflows, visualizations and working prototypes.
